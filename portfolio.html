01 — About

A little story.

I'm an Assistant Professor with expertise in 2D & 3D animation, VFX, rigging, graphic design and UI/UX design, teaching both the creative and the technical sides of digital media.

My skills span character animation, character design, 3D modelling, visual effects, compositing, motion graphics and user interface design.

I mentor students by combining creative thinking, technical proficiency and industry-oriented practice, preparing them for careers in animation, VFX, visual communication and digital design. I'm also pursuing a Ph.D. on a Human-Centered AI Design Framework for Professional UI/UX Workflows.
